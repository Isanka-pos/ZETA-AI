We aim to respond within minutes during business hours. For more complex technical issues, resolution may take up to 24 hours. Premium members receive priority response times.
Can I report bugs?
Absolutely! You can report any bugs or issues through our WhatsApp support channel or by contacting us directly. Please include a description of the issue and any relevant screenshots for faster resolution.

You can join our WhatsApp support channel by clicking the "Join WhatsApp Channel" button on this page. You'll get real-time updates, support, and community access directly through WhatsApp.

Yes! Premium members receive priority support with faster response times, dedicated assistance channels, and extended support hours. Upgrade to premium for the best ZETA TOOLS experience.
